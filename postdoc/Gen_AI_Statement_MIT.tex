I used generative AI for language polishing and editing, and as an aid in checking mathematical correctness, in the following three recent preprints:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02551}{\emph{Asymptotic behavior of the one-dimensional Vlasov--Poisson system with a repulsive harmonic potential}}.\par
This paper studies modified scattering for one-dimensional Vlasov--Poisson dynamics with a repulsive harmonic potential, including arbitrary-size data for repulsive self-interactions.

\item \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02643}{\emph{A common structure for modified scattering: Vlasov--Riesz, Hartree, and their coupling}}.\par
This paper develops a common action--density framework for modified scattering in strongly long-range Vlasov--Riesz, Hartree, and coupled Vlasov--Hartree equations.

\item \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04402}{\emph{Long-time dynamics of Vlasov--Hartree systems across the Coulomb threshold}}.\par
This paper establishes small-data global existence and describes scattering and modified scattering for three-dimensional Vlasov--Hartree systems with Coulomb and inverse-power interactions.
\end{enumerate}
