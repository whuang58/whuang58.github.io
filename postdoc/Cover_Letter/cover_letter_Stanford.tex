Dear Committee Members,

I am writing to apply for the Szeg\H{o} Assistant Professorship in the Department of Mathematics at Stanford University, beginning in September 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Beno\^{i}t Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in 2027.

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the long-time behavior and modified scattering of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems and Hartree equations, as well as the stability and instability of small BGK waves. Stanford is my top choice for a postdoctoral position, particularly because of the opportunity to work with Professor Jonathan Luk. His work on the Vlasov--Poisson system and related kinetic equations aligns closely with my research interests.

I have teaching experience at Brown University, which is described in my teaching statement, and I look forward to teaching at Stanford and contributing to the life of the Department.

Enclosed in my application, submitted via MathJobs, are a CV, a research statement, a teaching statement and a publication list. Five letters of recommendation will be submitted separately through MathJobs: four regarding my research from Professors Beno\^{i}t Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Klaus Widmayer and Yan Guo, and one regarding my teaching from Professor Yu-lin Lin. Further information and copies of my papers and preprints are available at my website:

\url{https://whuang58.github.io/}.
