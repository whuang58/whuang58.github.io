Dear Professor Iacobelli,

I am writing to apply for the postdoctoral position in Analysis and Partial Differential Equations in your group at ETH Z\"urich, starting in September 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Benoit Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in June 2027.

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the stability and long-time behavior of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems: the Vlasov--Poisson system in convex domains with a perfectly conducting wall, the Vlasov--Poisson system with a repulsive harmonic potential, the Vlasov--Riesz system, and the stability and instability of small BGK waves. These questions are closely related to the research of your group on plasma mathematics. In particular, I am also very interested  Vlasov--Maxwell systems.

I have teaching experience at Brown University and would be happy to contribute to the teaching duties of the position, including organizing a course.

Enclosed in my application (submitted via MathJobs) are a CV with a list of publications and a research statement. Additionally, you should receive three recommendation letters regarding my research, from Professors Benoit Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier and Klaus Widmayer.
