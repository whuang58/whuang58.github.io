Dear Committee Members,

I am writing to apply for the Gibbs Assistant Professorship in the Department of Mathematics at Yale University, beginning in July 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Beno\^{i}t Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in 2027.

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the long-time behavior and modified scattering of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems and Hartree equations, as well as the stability and instability of small BGK waves. I would very much like to work with Professor Wilhelm Schlag on the long-time behavior of nonlinear dispersive equations. His work on modified scattering and the dynamics of perturbations of solitons connects closely with my interests in scattering and stability. I have also presented my work at Yale's Analysis Seminar and the Brown--Yale PDE Seminar, and I would welcome the opportunity to contribute further to this community.

I have teaching experience at Brown University, which is described in my teaching statement, and I look forward to teaching at Yale and contributing to the life of the Department.

Enclosed in my application, submitted via Interfolio, are a CV, a research statement, a teaching statement and a publication list. Five letters of recommendation will be submitted separately through Interfolio: four regarding my research from Professors Beno\^{i}t Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Klaus Widmayer and Yan Guo, and one regarding my teaching from Professor Yu-lin Lin. Further information and copies of my papers and preprints are available at my website:

\url{https://whuang58.github.io/}.

Thank you for your time and consideration. Please let me know if you need any other information from me.
