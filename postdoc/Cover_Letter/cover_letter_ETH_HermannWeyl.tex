Dear Committee Members,
 
I am writing to apply for the Hermann-Weyl-Instructorship at the Institute for Mathematical Research (FIM) of ETH Z\"urich, starting in September 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Benoit Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in June 2027.
 
My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the stability and long-time behavior of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems: the Vlasov--Poisson system in convex domains with a perfectly conducting wall, the Vlasov--Poisson system with a repulsive harmonic potential, the Vlasov--Riesz system, and the stability and instability of small BGK waves. I have also worked on modified scattering for Hartree-type equations. These interests are shared by ETH's strong groups in analysis and partial differential equations. In particular, my work on the Vlasov--Poisson system and related kinetic models is closely related to the research of Professor Mikaela Iacobelli, and I would be very happy to work with her as my mentor. I would also benefit greatly from interacting with Professors Alessio Figalli and Hyunju Kwon.
 
I have teaching experience at Brown University and would be happy to supervise exercise classes at the Department.
 
Enclosed in my application (submitted via MathJobs) are a CV, a research statement, a teaching statement and a publication list. Additionally, you should receive four recommendation letters regarding my research (from Professors Benoit Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Klaus Widmayer and Yan Guo) and one regarding my teaching (from Professor Yu-lin Lin). Further information (and copies of papers/preprints) is available at my website:

\url{https://whuang58.github.io/}.
