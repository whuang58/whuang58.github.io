Dear Committee Members,

I am writing to apply for the Elliott and Griffiths Assistant Research Professorships in the Department of Mathematics at Duke University, beginning in August 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Beno\^{i}t Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in 2027.

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the long-time behavior and modified scattering of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems and Hartree equations, as well as the stability and instability of small BGK waves. I would welcome the opportunity to interact with Professors Jian-Guo Liu, Alexander Kiselev and Tarek Elgindi, whose work on nonlinear PDE and fluid dynamics connects with my broader research interests.

I have teaching experience at Brown University, which is described in my teaching statement, and I look forward to teaching at Duke and contributing to the life of the Department.

My application materials include a CV, a research statement and a teaching statement. My research referees are Professors Beno\^{i}t Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Klaus Widmayer and Yan Guo; Professor Yu-lin Lin is my teaching referee. Further information and copies of my papers and preprints are available at my website:

\url{https://whuang58.github.io/}.
