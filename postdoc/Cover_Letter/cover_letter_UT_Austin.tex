Dear Committee Members,

I am writing to apply for the R.\,H. Bing Faculty Fellowship and associated Instructorship in the Department of Mathematics at The University of Texas at Austin, beginning in Fall 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Beno\^{i}t Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in 2027.

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the long-time behavior and modified scattering of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems and Hartree equations, as well as the stability and instability of small BGK waves. I would very much like to work with Professor Nata\v{s}a Pavlovi\'{c}, whose research on the asymptotic dynamics of kinetic and nonlinear dispersive equations connects closely with my interests in scattering and long-time behavior. I would also welcome the opportunity to interact with Professor Thomas Chen on nonlinear dispersive equations and their connections with many-body dynamics.

I have teaching experience at Brown University, which is described in my teaching statement, and I look forward to teaching at UT Austin and contributing to the life of the Department.

Enclosed in my application, submitted via Interfolio, are a CV, a research statement and a teaching statement. Five letters of recommendation will be submitted separately through Interfolio: four regarding my research from Professors Beno\^{i}t Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Klaus Widmayer and Yan Guo, and one regarding my teaching from Professor Yu-lin Lin. Further information and copies of my papers and preprints are available at my website:

\url{https://whuang58.github.io/}.
