Dear Committee Members,
 
I am writing to apply for the Hale Visiting Assistant Professorship and the Visiting Assistant Professorship in the School of Mathematics at Georgia Tech, starting in Fall 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Benoit Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in June 2027.
 
My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. These interests are shared by many members of Georgia Tech's strong group in analysis and partial differential equations. In particular, my work on the Vlasov--Poisson system and on the stability of BGK waves is closely related to the research of Professor Chongchun Zeng. Furthermore, my work on dispersive and Hartree-type equations is related to the research of Professor Gong Chen. I would be very happy to work with them at Georgia Tech.
 
I have teaching experience at Brown University, which is described in my teaching statement, and I look forward to teaching at Georgia Tech.
 
Enclosed in my application (submitted via MathJobs) are a CV with a list of publications, a research statement, a teaching statement and an AMS cover sheet. Additionally, you should receive four recommendation letters regarding my research (from Professors Benoit Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Klaus Widmayer and Yan Guo) and one regarding my teaching (from Professor Yu-lin Lin).
