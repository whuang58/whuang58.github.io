Dear Committee Members,
 
I am writing to apply for the L.\,E. Dickson Instructorship at the University of Chicago, starting in Fall 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Benoit Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in June 2027.
 
My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. These interests are shared by many members of Chicago's strong groups in analysis and partial differential equations. In particular, my work on dispersive equations is related to the research of Professors Carlos Kenig and Yu Deng. Furthermore, my work on the Vlasov--Poisson system and kinetic theory is related to the research of Professor Luis Silvestre.
 
I have teaching experience at Brown University, which is described in my teaching statement, and I look forward to teaching at Chicago.
 
Enclosed in my application (submitted via MathJobs) are a CV, a research statement, a teaching statement and a publication list. Additionally, you should receive three recommendation letters regarding my research (from Professors Benoit Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Yan Guo and Klaus Widmayer) and one regarding my teaching (from Professor Yu-lin Lin). Further information (and copies of papers/preprints) is available at my website:
