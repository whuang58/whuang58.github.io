Dear Committee Members,

I am writing to apply for the Postdoctoral Research Associate position in Pure Mathematics (LF51540) in the Department of Pure Mathematics and Mathematical Statistics at the University of Cambridge, beginning in October 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Beno\^{i}t Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in 2027.

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the long-time behavior and modified scattering of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems and Hartree equations, as well as the stability and instability of small BGK waves. I am particularly interested in working with Professors Pierre Rapha\"{e}l and Cl\'{e}ment Mouhot. Professor Rapha\"{e}l's work on nonlinear wave dynamics and Professor Mouhot's work on Landau damping and stability of Vlasov equilibria offer close connections to my interests in long-time behavior and stability of kinetic and dispersive equations.

I would welcome the opportunity to develop an independent research program within the department's analysis community and to contribute to its seminars and research activities. I also have teaching experience at Brown University and would be glad to contribute to teaching and mentoring where opportunities arise.

My application materials include a CV, a description of my recent and planned research and a publication list. Academic referee contact details are provided in my application. Further information and copies of my papers and preprints are available at my website:

\url{https://whuang58.github.io/}.
