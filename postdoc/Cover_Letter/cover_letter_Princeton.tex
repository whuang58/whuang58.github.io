Dear Committee Members,

I am writing to apply for the postdoctoral and instructorship positions in the Department of Mathematics at Princeton University for the 2027--2028 academic year. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Beno\^{i}t Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in 2027.

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the long-time behavior and modified scattering of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems and Hartree equations, as well as the stability and instability of small BGK waves. These interests connect closely with Princeton's strong group in analysis and partial differential equations. In particular, I would very much like to work with Professor Alexandru Ionescu, whose research on the Vlasov--Poisson system, Landau damping and nonlinear dispersive equations is closely related to my own. I would also welcome the opportunity to interact with Professor Igor Rodnianski on nonlinear wave and dispersive equations.

I have teaching experience at Brown University, which is described in my teaching statement, and I look forward to teaching at Princeton and contributing to the life of the Department.

Enclosed in my application, submitted via MathJobs, are a CV, a research statement, a teaching statement and a publication list. Four letters of recommendation regarding my research, from Professors Beno\^{i}t Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Klaus Widmayer and Yan Guo, will be submitted separately through MathJobs. Further information and copies of my papers and preprints are available at my website:

\url{https://whuang58.github.io/}.
