Dear Committee Members,

I am writing to apply for a Postdoctoral Research Associate position in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Notre Dame, beginning in summer 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Beno\^{i}t Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in 2027.

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the long-time behavior and modified scattering of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems and Hartree equations, as well as the stability and instability of small BGK waves. I would welcome the opportunity to interact with Professors Matthew Novack and Alex Himonas and to participate in Notre Dame's PDE community.

I have teaching experience at Brown University, which is described in my teaching statement, and I look forward to teaching at Notre Dame and contributing to the life of the Department.

Enclosed in my application, submitted via MathJobs, are a CV, a research statement and a teaching statement. Five letters of recommendation will be submitted separately through MathJobs: four regarding my research from Professors Beno\^{i}t Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Klaus Widmayer and Yan Guo, and one regarding my teaching from Professor Yu-lin Lin. Further information and copies of my papers and preprints are available at my website:
