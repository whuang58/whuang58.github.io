Dear Committee Members,

I am writing to apply for the C.L.E.\ Moore Instructorship in the Department of Mathematics at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Beno\^{i}t Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in 2027.

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the long-time behavior and modified scattering of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems and Hartree equations, as well as the stability and instability of small BGK waves. I would welcome the opportunity to interact with Professor Gigliola Staffilani, whose work on nonlinear dispersive equations and scattering connects with my research interests.

I have teaching experience at Brown University, which is described in my teaching statement, and I look forward to teaching at MIT and contributing to the life of the Department.

My application materials include a CV, a research statement and a teaching statement. My research referees are Professors Beno\^{i}t Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Klaus Widmayer and Yan Guo; Professor Yu-lin Lin is my teaching referee. Further information and copies of my papers and preprints are available at my website:

\url{https://whuang58.github.io/}.
