Dear Committee Members,

I am writing to apply for the Joseph Fels Ritt Assistant Professorship in the Department of Mathematics at Columbia University, beginning in July 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Beno\^{i}t Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in 2027.

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the long-time behavior and modified scattering of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems and Hartree equations, as well as the stability and instability of small BGK waves. I would welcome the opportunity to interact with Professor Michael I. Weinstein. His work on nonlinear scattering and the stability of solitary waves connects closely with my interests in the asymptotic dynamics of nonlinear dispersive equations.

I have teaching experience at Brown University, which is described in my teaching statement, and I look forward to teaching at Columbia and contributing to the life of the Department.

My application materials include a CV, a research statement, a teaching statement and a publication list. My research referees are Professors Beno\^{i}t Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Klaus Widmayer and Yan Guo; Professor Yu-lin Lin is my teaching referee. Further information and copies of my papers and preprints are available at my website:

\url{https://whuang58.github.io/}.
