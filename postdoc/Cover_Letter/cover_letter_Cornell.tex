Dear Committee Members,

I am writing to apply for the H.C.\ Wang Assistant Professorship in the Department of Mathematics at Cornell University, beginning in July 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Beno\^{i}t Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in 2027.

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the long-time behavior and modified scattering of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems and Hartree equations, as well as the stability and instability of small BGK waves. I am particularly interested in working with Professor Sanchit Chaturvedi. His work on phase mixing for Vlasov--Poisson equations with external potentials connects closely with my interests in how external forces influence long-time kinetic dynamics.

I have teaching experience at Brown University, which is described in my teaching statement, and I look forward to teaching at Cornell and contributing to the life of the Department.

Enclosed in my application, submitted via MathJobs, are a CV, a research statement and a teaching statement. My research referees are Professors Beno\^{i}t Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Klaus Widmayer and Yan Guo; Professor Yu-lin Lin will provide a teaching reference. Further information and copies of my papers and preprints are available at my website:

\url{https://whuang58.github.io/}.
