Dear Committee Members,
 
I am writing to apply for the Brin/Novikov Postdoctoral Fellowship  at the
University of Maryland, College Park, starting in Fall 2027. I am currently a Ph.D student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Benoit Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D in June, 2027. 
 
My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations.  These interests are shared by many members of Maryland's strong groups in analysis and partial differential equations. In particular, my work on dispersive and Hartree-type equations is related to the research of Professors Manoussos Grillakis, Matei Machedon and Andrew Lawrie. Furthermore, my work on the Vlasov--Poisson system is related to some of the research of Professors Eitan Tadmor, Christopher Henderson and Antoine Mellet.
 
Enclosed in my application (submitted via mathjobs), is a CV, a research
statement, a teaching statement and a publication list. Additionally, you
should receive three recommendation letters regarding my research (from
Professors Benoit Pausader, Emmanuel Grenier, Klaus Widmayer) and one
regarding my teaching (from Professor Yu-lin Lin). Further information
(and copies of papers/preprints) is available at my website:

\url{https://whuang58.github.io/}.
