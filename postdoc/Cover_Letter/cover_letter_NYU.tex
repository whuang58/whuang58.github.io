Dear Committee Members,

I am writing to apply for the Courant Instructorship in the Department of Mathematics at New York University, beginning in Fall 2027. I am currently a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Brown University, working under the supervision of Professor Beno\^{i}t Pausader, and I expect to receive my Ph.D. in 2027.

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations, kinetic theory and dispersive equations. Much of my work concerns the long-time behavior and modified scattering of Vlasov--Poisson-type systems and Hartree equations, as well as the stability and instability of small BGK waves. I would very much like to work with Professor Jalal Shatah on the long-time behavior of nonlinear dispersive equations. His work in this area, including joint work with Nader Masmoudi, connects closely with my interests in scattering and asymptotic dynamics. Courant's active community in analysis and partial differential equations would be an excellent environment in which to develop these research directions.

I have teaching experience at Brown University, which is described in my teaching statement, and I look forward to teaching at NYU and contributing to the life of the Department.

Enclosed in my application, submitted via Interfolio, are a CV with a publication list, a research statement and a teaching statement. Three letters of recommendation will be submitted separately through Interfolio: two regarding my research from Professors Yan Guo and Beno\^{i}t Pausader, and one regarding my teaching from Professor Yu-lin Lin. Further information and copies of my papers and preprints are available at my website:

\url{https://whuang58.github.io/}.

Thank you for your time and consideration. Please let me know if you need any other information from me.
